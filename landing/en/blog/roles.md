In Martha, sit, what someone can do depends on their **Church role**. Most team members don’t need one. Give someone a church role when you want them to help arrange serving or look after team members. One person can have several.

## The church roles

- **Admin:** Gives church roles, changes the church’s settings, and removes team members from the church. An admin can also do whatever the other roles can.
- **Pastor:** When you give it, you pick **Pastor of**. Pick **Whole church**, and they’re the same as an admin. Pick services, and they’re a lead of those services.
- **Office:** Arranges every schedule, for services and events, edits every event on the Calendar, and sees all attendance. They can also change the name, services, roles and small groups of any team member who isn’t an admin. They can’t give church roles, remove team members, or change settings.
- **Service lead:** Arranges that service’s schedule, changes who is on the lists for its roles, adds and removes its team members, and can add small groups to it.
- **Ministry lead:** Arranges only the roles linked to their ministry. For example, the worship team lead arranges Worship Leader, Piano, and Drums, and can change who is on those lists.
- **Small group leader:** Adds and removes the group’s team members, sets when it meets, and fills in attendance.

Admin, Pastor and Office are given on a team member’s page. Leads and small group leaders are assigned on the page of what they lead.

## Give Admin, Pastor or Office

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team members** and tap a team member.
2. Under **Church role**, tap **Add a church role**, then pick **Admin**, **Pastor**, or **Office**.
3. If you picked **Pastor**, pick **Pastor of** next: **Whole church**, or one service. For several services, add **Pastor** again and pick another service.

![Tap Add a church role, then pick Admin, Pastor or Office](shot:roles-add)

## Assign leads and small group leaders

- **Service lead:** Go to **Settings** → **Services & ministries**, open the service, and under **Leads**, tap **Assign a lead**.
- **Ministry lead:** Open the ministry’s page and tap **Assign a lead** there too.
- **From a role:** On a service’s page, tap **Roles**, then a role, like Slides. At the top, under **Leads**, tap **Assign a lead**. If the role isn’t in a ministry yet, a ministry called Slides is made with just that role, and the person you pick becomes its lead. If the role is already in a ministry, like the worship team, the person you pick becomes a lead of the worship team.
- **Small group leader:** Open the small group’s page, tap **Small group leader**, and pick a team member. A small group has one leader. A service’s leads and pastors can also assign the leaders of its small groups.

![The Slides page: under Leads, tap Assign a lead](shot:roles-duty-lead)

A service or ministry can have several leads, all with the same permissions: tap **Add another lead**. For what they do once assigned, see [Leads: add team members and arrange the serving you lead](/en/blog/for-leads/) and [Small group leaders: set when you meet and fill in attendance](/en/blog/small-groups/).

## Take a role away

- On a team member’s page, under **Church role**, tap **Remove** next to it. Leads and small group leaders are listed here too, and can be removed the same way.
- On a service’s or ministry’s page, tap **Remove** next to a lead. For anyone marked **Pastor** there, change it on their team member page.
- For a small group leader, tap **Small group leader** on the group’s page, then **Unassign**.

Tapped it by mistake? Tap **Undo** to bring it back.

On your own page, you can’t remove the last church role that makes you an admin (**Admin** or **Whole church pastor**). To hand it over, give the other person **Admin** first, then ask them to remove yours.
