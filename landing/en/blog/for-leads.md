At many churches, one person doesn’t schedule everything. The Youth Service has its own lead, and the worship team and sound team each schedule their own people. In Martha, sit, leads arrange their own serving, without going through an admin every time.

## Who’s a lead

An admin assigns leads. There are two kinds:

- **Service lead:** Arranges a service’s whole schedule, like the Youth Service lead.
- **Ministry lead:** Arranges only the roles linked to their ministry. For example, the worship team lead schedules Worship Leader, Piano, and Drums. The other roles still show, but they can’t be changed.

An admin can go to **Settings** → **Team members**, tap a team member, and add a lead role under **Church role**. Or go to **Settings** → **Services & ministries**, open a service or ministry, and tap **Assign a lead**. A service or ministry can have several leads, all with the same permissions.

## Start with “Get started”

Once you’re assigned, a **Get started** card for your role shows up at the top of Home, like **Get started · Youth Service lead**. It explains what you can do and lists two things to do first:

1. **Add team members:** Add the people who serve with you.
2. **Arrange the schedule:** Schedule the serving you lead.

Each one disappears once it’s done. If you’d rather not see the card, tap **Don’t show again**.

## Add team members

Go to **Settings** → **I lead** and tap the service or ministry you lead to open its page:

- Under **Team members**, tap **Add team members**, pick people from the whole church list, and tap to add them.
- When someone stops serving, tap **Remove** next to their name. Tapped it by mistake? Tap **Undo** to bring them back.
- People who came to help and have served here but aren’t team members yet are listed under **Served with Youth Service, not on the team yet**. Tap **Add to team** to add them.

![On the small group page, tap Add team members and pick two people to add](media:addStaff)

The video shows a small group’s page. Service and ministry pages work the same way.

Once they’re added:

- **A service’s team members** can see that service’s schedule, and they show up in the list when you pick people.
- **A ministry’s team members** can see the ministry’s events when they choose **Related to me** in the Calendar.

## Arrange serving

Go to **Schedule** and tap **Arrange serving** at the top right. It puts several weeks in one table. When you’re finished, tap **Done**, and each team member gets just one notification.

![Tap Host for two weeks, pick someone for each, then tap Done to notify the team](media:plan)

For how to arrange and swap, see [Arrange serving: several weeks at once, drag to swap](/en/blog/plan-weeks/).

## What leads can’t do

For these, you’ll still need an admin:

- Give team members a church role, or remove someone who has one (such as another lead).
- Change service settings, like adding a role or changing which days you have services.
- Ministry leads can’t change occasions or reorder the list of people to pick from.
