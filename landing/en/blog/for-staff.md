description: Once you’ve joined with an invite link, here’s how to see when you serve on Home, ask for a cover or swap weeks when you can’t make it, and mark the days you can’t serve when a lead asks.

This guide is for team members who joined with an invite link. There’s nothing to set up. Open the app and your serving is there.

Right after you join, if Home shows a welcome card and no serving, an admin hasn’t added you to a service yet. Once they do, you’ll see the schedule. If your name was already on the schedule, tap **Already on the schedule?** on the card, pick which one is you, and tap **Send**. Once an admin confirms, the serving already arranged for that name becomes yours.

## Home

**When you’re serving** lists each day you’re scheduled, in date order, with the service and the role. Days from different services are listed together.

![Home lists when you’re serving and upcoming events](shot:home-still)

Tap a day to open that day’s schedule and see who else is serving with you. Your name is in bold.

To see several weeks at once, go to **Schedule** and tap **Table** at the top right. The **Schedule** tab only has the services you’re on. If you also serve at another service, it’s still listed on Home and you can open it. To see that service under **Schedule**, ask an admin or that service’s lead to add you as a team member.

## Can’t make it: ask for a cover

At the top of that day’s schedule is **You’re on**, with **I can’t make it** on the right. Tap it, and the app asks “Ask for a cover for Host on Nov 22?” Tap **Ask**.

![That day’s schedule, with You’re on and I can’t make it at the top](shot:staff-day)

- It goes to the team members on that role’s list who are free that day, one notification each. Whoever taps **I’ll do it** first takes it. The schedule changes to their name, and you and the lead are both notified.
- Until someone takes it, you’re still on the schedule. Your row says **Asked for a cover; nobody has taken it yet**. If you no longer need it, tap **Take back**.
- If nobody else on the list is free that day, the leads and the others serving that day are told you can’t make it instead. You stay on the schedule, and they sort it out.
- If nobody has taken it three days before, the lead is notified.

## Swap with another week

Tap your own row on the schedule (it has ⋯ on the right) and choose **Swap with another week**. You’ll see the people on that role on later days. Tap one, and only that person is asked. If they tap **Accept**, the two days are swapped, and you and the lead are notified. If they tap **Can’t**, you’re notified too.

You can’t ask for a cover or swap on an event’s schedule or on a day that’s past. For those, talk to the lead.

## When someone asks you

You get a notification, and **Waiting on you** shows up at the top of Home. Tap it to see which day and role. For a swap, it shows who takes which day.

- Tap **I’ll do it** (for a swap, **Accept**) and it’s done. That day’s schedule opens. Tapped it by mistake? Tap **Undo** right away.
- If you can’t, tap **Can’t**. Saying **Can’t** to a cover request doesn’t notify anyone, and others can still take it.

## Days you can’t serve

Go to **Settings** → **Days I can’t serve**. Tap a day to mark it, and tap again to clear it. Drag your finger across several days to mark them all at once. Each tap is saved right away. You can fill these in any time, without being asked.

- **Days I can’t** lists the days you marked. Tap one to add a reason, like a trip abroad.
- Only the people who arrange serving can see them. When they schedule, your name still shows, with **Can’t serve this day**.
- If you’re already scheduled on a day you mark, you aren’t taken off. It’s listed under **You’re already serving on these days**. Tap **Find a cover or swap** next to it.

![A few days marked as days you can’t serve, with the serving already on one of them listed below](shot:staff-unavailable)

Before arranging serving, a lead may ask everyone to fill these in. You get a notification, and Home shows **Mark days you can’t serve**, with the months to fill in on the right, like Dec–Jan. Tap it to open on that month. Mark your days, then tap **Send** at the bottom. If you can serve on every day in those months, the button says **I can serve every day**. Tap it the same way. Once you’ve sent it, the row on Home goes away. You can still change your days later, and there’s no need to send again.

## More

- **Notifications:** You’re notified when you’re put on or taken off serving, or when someone asks you to cover. Not getting them, or want to change when serving reminders come? See [Not getting notifications?](/en/blog/notifications/)
- **Calendar:** If your church has connected a calendar, the top right of **Calendar** says **Related to me**: the whole church’s events, events of the services, ministries and small groups you’re in, and events you’re serving at. Tap it and pick **All** to see every event.
- **Serving at another church too:** Join with that church’s invite link, then go to **Settings** → **Church info** and tap **Switch church**.
- **Leaving the church:** Go to **Settings** → **Church info**, tap **Leave church**, then tap **Leave church** again. To come back, you’ll need a new invite.
