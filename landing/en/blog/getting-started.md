This guide is for admins using Martha, sit for the first time, from signing in to having your team join your church.

## 1. Sign in

Open the [web app](/home) and choose **Sign in with Google**.

No Google account? Choose **Sign in with email**, then tap **Create account**. If you sign up with email, open the verification email in your inbox first, then come back and tap **I’ve verified**. After that, you can create a church.

## 2. Create your church

After you sign in, you’ll see **Join your church**. Choose **Create a church**, enter your church’s name, and tap **Create**.

Whoever creates the church becomes its admin.

## 3. Invite team members

Once your church is set up, you’ll see **Get started** at the top of Home. Start with **Invite team members**.

Choose **Valid for 7 days** or **Valid for 30 days**. This creates an invite link and opens the share menu, so you can send it straight to your team’s group chat. If your browser doesn’t have a share menu, the link is copied for you. Just paste it into the group chat.

Invites you’ve created are listed below. Tap an invite code to **Share** it again, **Copy link**, or **Revoke** it.

## 4. Your team joins

Team members open the invite link, sign in, and tap **Join**. That’s it, they’re in your church.

If the link won’t open, they can sign in, choose **Enter invite code** on the **Join your church** screen, and type the invite code or paste the whole link.

## What’s next

Go to **Service settings** to set which days you have services each week and which roles need people. Then you’re ready to start planning the schedule.

Until someone joins, **Service settings** is right there under **Get started** on Home. After that, you can find it under **Me** → **Church info** → **Service settings**.
