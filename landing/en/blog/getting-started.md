This guide is for admins using Martha, sit for the first time, from signing in to having your team join your church.

## 1. Sign in

Open the [web app](/home) and choose **Sign in with Google**.

No Google account? Choose **Sign in with email**, then tap **Create account**. If you sign up with email, open the verification email in your inbox first, then come back and tap **I’ve verified**. After that, you can create a church.

## 2. Create your church

After you sign in, you’ll see **Join your church**. Choose **Create a church**, enter your church’s name, and tap **Create**.

Whoever creates the church becomes its admin. Your new church comes with a Sunday Service and common roles already set up. Use them as they are, or change them later.

## 3. Service settings

At the top of Home, **Get started** lists what to do next. Start with **Service settings** and check which days you have services each week and which roles need people, such as Host, Piano, and Sound.

To change these later, go to **Settings** → **Service settings**.

## 4. Invite team members

Back in **Get started**, tap **Invite team members**.

- **Joins these services:** Check which services new team members belong to, and they’ll see those services’ schedules. You can leave these unchecked and set them later in **Team members**.
- **Valid for:** Links are **Valid for 7 days** unless you choose **Valid for 30 days**.

Tap **Create invite link** to open the share menu, so you can send it straight to your team’s group chat. If your browser doesn’t have a share menu, the link is copied for you. Just paste it into the group chat.

![Tap Create invite link, and the link is copied](media:invite)

Invites you’ve created are listed below. Tap an invite code to **Share** it again, **Copy link**, or **Revoke** it. To invite more people later, go to **Settings** → **Invites**.

## 5. Your team joins

Team members open the invite link, sign in, and tap **Join**. That’s it, they’re in your church.

If the link won’t open, they can sign in, choose **Enter invite code** on the **Join your church** screen, and type the invite code or paste the whole link.

Team members who join from a phone’s browser will then see how to **Add to Home Screen**. On iPhone, they need to open the app from the Home Screen icon to get notifications. See [Not getting notifications?](/en/blog/notifications/)

## What’s next

- Back in **Get started**, tap **Arrange the schedule**, then tap **Arrange serving** at the top right of **Schedule** to arrange several weeks at once and notify your team when you’re done. See [Arrange serving: several weeks at once, drag to swap](/en/blog/plan-weeks/).
- Want your worship team and sound team leads to arrange their own serving? Give them a church role in **Settings** → **Team members**. See [Leads: add team members and arrange the serving you lead](/en/blog/for-leads/).
