This guide is for admins using Martha, sit for the first time, from signing in to having your team join your church.

## 1. Sign in

Choose **Sign in with Google**<span data-from-app="hide"> in the [web app](/home)</span>.

No Google account? Choose **Sign in with email**, then tap **Create account**. If you sign up with email, open the verification email in your inbox first and tap its link. The app moves on by itself, and then you can create a church.

## 2. Create your church

After you sign in, you’ll see **Join your church**. Choose **Create a church**, enter your church’s name, check that **Your name** below is what your team calls you, and tap **Create**.

Whoever creates the church becomes its admin. Your new church comes with a Sunday Service and common roles already set up. Use them as they are, or change them later.

## 3. Get started

At the top of Home, **Get started** lists what to do next, in order. The blue one is next, what’s done gets checked off, and anything marked optional can be skipped.

![Get started on Home: Set up services, Arrange the schedule, Upload logo, Invite team members](shot:start-home)

## 4. Set up services

Tap **Set up services**, then Sunday Service, and check which day you meet each week and which roles need people, such as Host, Piano, and Sound. Swipe left to delete roles you don’t need.

![Sunday Service settings: the day and the roles](shot:start-service)

When you’re done, go back and tap **Next: Arrange the schedule** at the bottom. To change these later, go to **Settings** → **Services & ministries**.

## 5. Arrange the schedule

You can arrange people before they join. Tap **Arrange serving** at the top right of **Schedule**, tap a cell, type the team member’s name in the search box, and choose the first row to add them as a team member. Once they join, the serving you arranged is theirs. For someone helping just once, choose the next row to fill them in just this once.

![Type a name when picking to add a team member who hasn’t joined yet](shot:start-arrange)

When you’re finished, tap **Done** at the top right. For arranging several weeks at once and swapping, see [Arrange serving: several weeks at once, drag to swap](/en/blog/plan-weeks/).

**Upload logo** is optional. Once uploaded, your church’s logo shows on the invite page and Home. You can change it later in **Settings** → **Church info**.

## 6. Invite team members

Back in **Get started**, tap **Invite team members**.

- **Team member of these services:** Sunday Service is already checked, so new team members see its schedule once they join. For a service that isn’t checked, they won’t see it until you add them to it.
- **Valid for:** Links are **Valid for 7 days** unless you choose **Valid for 30 days**.

Tap **Create invite link** to open the share menu, so you can send it straight to your team’s LINE group. If your browser doesn’t have a share menu, the link is copied for you. Just paste it into the LINE group.

![Tap Create invite link, and the link is copied](shot:start-invite)

Invites you’ve created are listed below. Tap an invite code to **Share** it again, **Copy link**, or **Revoke** it so the link no longer works. To invite more people later, go to **Settings** → **Invites**.

**Requests to join** below starts out on: anyone with the **Church URL** can also ask to join, and they’re in once you accept. If you’d rather not, turn it off.

## 7. Your team joins

Team members open the invite link, sign in, check **Your name** (the name everyone sees on rosters, so they can change it to what the church calls them), and tap **Join**. That’s it, they’re in your church.

If you typed their name in when arranging serving, they’re asked when they join whether they’re Isaac Wang on the schedules. Tapping **That’s me** brings over the serving already arranged for them.

If the link won’t open, they can sign in, choose **Enter invite code** on the **Join your church** screen, and type the invite code or paste the whole link.

Team members who join from a phone’s browser will then see how to **Add to Home Screen**. On iPhone, they need to open the app from the Home Screen icon to get notifications. See [Not getting notifications?](/en/blog/notifications/)

## What’s next

- Want your worship team and sound team leads to arrange their own serving? Give them a church role in **Settings** → **Team members**. See [Leads: add team members and arrange the serving you lead](/en/blog/for-leads/).
- Have small groups? Tap **Create small groups** in **Get started**, and small group leaders can add their own members and fill in attendance. See [Small group leaders: set when you meet and fill in attendance](/en/blog/small-groups/).
