Churches like to know how many came to each small group meeting. Often that meant the leader texting the group chat and someone copying it into a spreadsheet. With Martha, sit, small group leaders fill it in right in the app, and if it’s not filled in by the evening of the meeting, they get a reminder.

## An admin sets things up first

1. In **Settings**, turn on **Attendance**.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Services & ministries**, open the service the small group belongs to, tap **Add a small group**, and give it a name.
3. On the small group’s page, tap **Small group leader** and pick a team member.

## Small group leaders start with “Get started”

Once you’re assigned, a section like **Get started · Joy Group small group leader** shows up at the top of Home, listing what to do first. To find your small group’s page later, go to **Settings** → **I lead**.

## Add members

On the small group’s page, under **Team members**, tap **Add team members**, pick people from the whole church list, and tap to add them. Added the wrong person? Tap **Remove** next to their name, or tap **Undo**.

![On the small group page, tap Add team members and pick two people to add to the group](media:addStaff)

## Set when you meet

Tap **Meets**, choose **Every week** or **Every other week**, pick the day, and tap **Save**.

![Tap Meets, choose every Thursday, then tap Save](media:meets)

Once that’s set, the **Attendance** section lists each meeting day. Days that haven’t come yet don’t show.

## Fill in attendance

On or after the meeting day, tap the meeting marked **Not filled in**, enter how many came, add a note if you like, and tap **Save**.

![Tap a meeting that isn’t filled in, enter 9 people, and save](media:attendance)

- **Didn’t meet that time:** Tap **We didn't meet**, so it doesn’t stay **Not filled in**.
- **Still not filled in by 9 p.m. on the meeting day:** The small group leader gets a reminder. To stop these, go to **Settings** → **Notifications** and turn off **Attendance reminders**.
- **Taking time off:** Tap **Take a break** and pick the day you’ll be back. There are no reminders during the break, and nothing is left **Not filled in**.

Admins and office staff can see the attendance you fill in, and it’s kept even after the small group is closed.
