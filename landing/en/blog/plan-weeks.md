This guide is for whoever plans the schedule: admins, office staff, and service or ministry leads. Opening one week at a time is slow. **Arrange serving** puts several weeks in one table, so you can do it all in one go.

## Open “Arrange serving”

Go to **Schedule** and tap the table icon at the top right. That’s **Arrange serving**.

- Each row is a day, and each column is a role. If there are a lot of roles, swipe sideways to see the other columns.
- Switch between services at the top, such as Sunday Service and Youth Service.
- It opens on the first month that still has open spots. To see earlier months, tap **Show earlier months**. As you scroll down, later months keep appearing, up to the same month next year.

Ministry leads can only change their own ministry’s roles. The other columns still show, so you can see the whole picture.

## Schedule people

Tap a cell to bring up the list of people:

- At the top are people who have served in this role, with how many times they’re already scheduled this month and anything else they’re doing that day, so no one gets scheduled too often.
- Below them are the other team members. You can also search by name.
- For roles that take one person (like Host or Piano), tap a name and you’re set. To schedule two or more people, tap **Add another person**, make your picks, then tap **Done**.

![Tap Host for two weeks, pick someone for each, then tap Done to notify the team](media:plan)

## Swap two weeks

Press and hold a cell, drag it to another week of the same role, and let go. The two weeks’ people swap places. Made a mistake? Tap **Undo**.

![Press and hold one week’s Host, drag it to the next week, and let go to swap the two people](media:swap)

You can also tap a cell, choose **Swap with another week** in the list of people, then pick the week to swap with.

## Tap “Done” when you’re finished

Changes you make here don’t notify anyone right away. When you’re finished, tap **Done** at the top right. Everyone added or removed gets one notification, not one for every cell. Every service you switched to is included.

- If you tap **Done** after 10 p.m., notifications wait until 7 a.m., so no one gets woken up in the middle of the night.
- If you leave without tapping **Done**, the Schedule reminds you that **People put on or taken off here haven't been notified**. Tap **Notify** to send them.
