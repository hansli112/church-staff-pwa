Just what your team needs

Made for the people who serve. The rest of the congregation doesn’t need to install anything.

See when you serve

Home lists your upcoming serving, and you get a notification when you’re put on the schedule.

Plan the schedule together

Admins and the team members in charge can all edit it. When you pick someone, the list follows your team’s order. You can also import a paper schedule from a photo.

One schedule, everywhere

Works on iPhone, Android, and the web. Make a change anywhere, and it syncs everywhere.

Get started in three steps

Try the web app first. No install needed.

  1. Create your churchAn admin signs in, creates the church, and sets up the roles.
  2. Send the invite linkShare the invite link in your team’s group chat.
  3. Your team joinsThey open the link, sign in with Google, and they’re in.

Free to use

No fees, and no paid features.

We all share the server costs. If you’d like to help, you can support us here, or leave a tip or subscribe in the iPhone and Android apps. Every feature is the same either way.
