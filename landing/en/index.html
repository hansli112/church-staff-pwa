Martha, sit

A serving schedule for your church team.

Free. On a computer, use the web app.

See when you serve

Home lists your upcoming serving, and you get a notification when you’re scheduled.

  • Calendar

    Your church’s Google Calendar, right in the app.

  • Arrange serving

    Several weeks in one table. Drag a cell onto another week to swap them.

  • Attendance

    Small group leaders fill in how many came, with a reminder if they forget.

  • Invites

    Send a link to your team’s group chat. They open it and join.
