Martha, sit (馬大別忙, run by Hsueh-Han Lee) is a serving schedule tool for church team members. You can delete your account and related data at any time.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Martha, sit app or the web app and sign in with your account.
  2. Tap Settings at the bottom.
  3. Tap your name at the top, then Delete account, and confirm.

If you’re the only admin of a church, first go to Team members and make someone else an admin. Then you can delete your account.

Can’t sign in? Email us

From the email you signed up with, send a message to support@marthasit.com with the subject “Delete my Martha, sit account,” and include the email you sign in with. Once we’ve confirmed it’s you, we’ll delete your account within 30 days and email you back.

What gets deleted

  • your sign-in account;
  • your account data, including your name, email, language setting, and the push tokens for all your devices;
  • your team member data in every church (status, permission groups, the serving you’re assigned to, notification settings).

What’s kept

  • Names on schedules: Names already on schedules are kept as plain text, because they’re part of the church’s serving records. To have them removed, contact that church’s admin.
  • Error logs and usage analytics: These don’t include your name or email, and they’re deleted automatically after their retention periods (up to 90 days for error logs and up to 14 months for usage analytics; see Section 3 of the Privacy Policy).
  • Transaction records: Payment records for one-time and monthly support in the app are kept by Apple or Google under their own rules. To stop monthly support, cancel the subscription in the App Store or Google Play.

Only want to delete some of your data?

  • Leave a church: Leave it yourself in the app. This deletes your team member data in that church and keeps your account.
  • Other data: Email us at the address above and tell us which data to delete.

For the full details, see the Privacy Policy.
