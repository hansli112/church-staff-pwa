Features

Every team member

See when you serve

Home lists your upcoming serving. Tap a day to see who’s serving with you. You get a notification when you’re scheduled.

Not getting notifications?

Every team member

Your church’s events, in the Calendar

Connect your church’s Google Calendar, and its events show up in the app. One-off events like a Christmas party or a retreat can have their own serving schedule.

People who arrange serving

Arrange several weeks at once

Arrange serving puts several weeks in one table. If someone can’t make a week, drag their cell to another week and the two weeks swap.

How to arrange serving

Small group leaders

Fill in attendance

If no one has filled it in by 9 p.m. on the meeting day, the small group leader gets a reminder. You can also note when you didn’t meet.

A guide for small group leaders

Admins

Send one link, and your team joins

Create an invite link and send it to your team’s LINE group. Team members open it and sign in to join.

Getting started: create your church and invite your team
