Welcome to Martha, sit (the “Service”). The Service is run by an individual, Hsueh-Han Lee (the “Operator,” “we,” or “us”). By using the Service, you agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy.

1. The Service

The Service is an online serving schedule tool that lets church team members view and plan schedules. It’s available as an iOS app, an Android app, and a web app.

The Service is designed for churches, for team members only, not for the wider congregation. Groups that aren’t churches (such as organizations or fellowships) may use it too, but its features and wording are built around churches, and we won’t adapt it for other uses.

2. Accounts

  • You can sign up with a Google account, an Apple account (iOS), or email.
  • Please use your real name and keep your sign-in method secure. Anything done with your account is treated as done by you.
  • Whoever creates a church becomes its admin. Other team members join through an admin’s invite link.

  • Payments, renewals, and subscription cancellations are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms. Please request refunds from Apple or Google; the Operator can’t issue refunds directly.

Support payments generally aren’t refundable; if you entered the wrong amount or were charged twice, email support@marthasit.com with the time and amount of the payment, and once we’ve checked, we’ll refund it in full.

5. Admin responsibilities

Each church’s admins are responsible for managing their church’s team member data. Admins agree to:

  • get a person’s consent, or have another lawful basis, before adding them as a team member or putting them on the schedule;
  • use team members’ data (such as email) only as needed for scheduling, and not for anything else;
  • help when a team member asks to correct or delete their own data;
  • give admin rights only to people they trust.

For team member data and schedules, the church (through its admins) decides whose data is collected and for what purpose; the Operator provides the system according to the church’s settings and stores and processes this data on the church’s behalf.

To deal with abuse, violations of these terms, name squatting, or disputes, the Operator may, without prior notice:

  • rename a church;

No two churches can have the same name: creating a church with an existing church’s name is blocked. If someone else has already registered your church’s name, please contact us, and we’ll handle it after looking into it.

Please keep your own backup of important serving schedules.

8. Deleting accounts and churches

  • Leaving a church: If you’re not an admin, you can leave yourself in the app. To come back, you’ll need a new invite.
  • Deleting your account: You can delete your account yourself in the app. This deletes your sign-in account, your account data, and your team member status in every church. Names already on schedules are kept as plain text. If you’re the only admin of a church, you need to hand it over to someone else first.
  • Deleting a church: A deleted church is kept for 30 days and can be restored during that time. After 30 days, it’s permanently deleted.

For exactly what gets deleted, see the Privacy Policy. Without the app, you can also make a request by following How to delete your account.
